# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

This guide summarizes how OctoAcme initiates, plans, delivers, and improves projects. Use the linked process documents for detailed guidance and templates.

## Project management process

OctoAcme starts by validating a project’s business need, measurable outcomes, stakeholders, risks, and resources. Once success measures, priority, and team availability are clear, the team plans shippable increments, prioritizes a backlog with acceptance criteria and estimates, defines the Definition of Done, and agrees on dependencies, milestones, and a release plan.

During execution, teams track work on a project board and use regular standups, delivery syncs, and demos to surface progress, blockers, and risks. The Project Manager coordinates delivery, schedules, risks, and communication; the Product Manager owns outcomes and priorities; developers build and test; QA validates acceptance; and stakeholders provide input and approvals. Risks are recorded, reviewed, mitigated, and escalated through the PM and Product Lead to the sponsor when needed.

Quality is supported by automated tests and linting in CI, unit and applicable integration tests, security scanning, manual QA when needed, and smoke tests for critical flows before release. Releases also require acceptance criteria, release notes, post-deployment checks, and a rollback or mitigation plan. After sprints, releases, milestones, or incidents, retrospectives turn lessons into a small set of owned, time-bound improvement actions.
