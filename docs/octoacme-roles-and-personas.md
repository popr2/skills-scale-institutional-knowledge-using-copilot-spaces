# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

The following are potential additional roles; projects can adapt them to their size and context. One person may cover multiple roles, but each responsibility and decision should have a clear owner.

## Product Lead / Business Owner

### Role Summary
The Product Lead or Business Owner is accountable for the business outcome and ensures the project remains aligned with organizational goals.

### Responsibilities
- Clarify the business need, expected outcomes, and success measures
- Make or secure timely business decisions and approve significant scope trade-offs
- Align sponsors and stakeholders on priorities and expected value

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Partners with Product Managers to translate business outcomes into product direction and priorities
- Aligns with Project Managers on scope decisions, delivery trade-offs, and escalations
- Provides Developers and other delivery roles with context when business decisions affect implementation

---

## Engineering Lead / Technical Lead

### Role Summary
The Engineering Lead guides technical design and engineering execution so the solution is feasible, maintainable, and aligned with project goals.

### Responsibilities
- Coordinate technical design and identify architecture or integration decisions
- Support estimation and sequencing of engineering work
- Surface technical dependencies, quality concerns, and delivery risks
- Promote maintainable implementation and appropriate engineering practices

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Works with Developers on design, implementation, reviews, and technical problem-solving
- Shares estimates, dependencies, and risks with Project Managers for planning and tracking
- Collaborates with Product Managers on technical trade-offs that affect scope or user outcomes
- Coordinates with QA, Security, and Operations roles on testability and readiness

---

## QA / Test Engineer

### Role Summary
The QA or Test Engineer helps the team verify that the product meets acceptance criteria and quality expectations.

### Responsibilities
- Plan test coverage, including integration, regression, and critical-flow testing
- Identify quality risks and document defects with clear reproduction details
- Validate fixes and report test results and outstanding risks before release

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Works with Product Managers to clarify acceptance criteria and expected behavior
- Partners with Developers to improve testability, investigate defects, and verify fixes
- Coordinates with Project Managers on test status, quality risks, and release readiness

---

## UX / Product Designer

### Role Summary
The UX or Product Designer researches user needs and shapes usable, accessible product experiences.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct or synthesize user research and communicate relevant findings
- Create and validate user flows, interaction designs, and interface guidance
- Identify usability and accessibility considerations early in delivery

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Partners with Product Managers to connect user needs to product goals and requirements
- Works with Developers to clarify designs and resolve implementation trade-offs
- Collaborates with QA on usability and accessibility checks against the intended experience

---

## Security / Privacy Lead

### Role Summary
The Security or Privacy Lead helps identify and reduce security and privacy risks across the project lifecycle.

### Responsibilities
- Advise on applicable security and privacy requirements
- Review relevant designs and risks, and recommend proportionate mitigations
- Clarify escalation paths and security or privacy readiness concerns

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Partners with the Engineering Lead and Developers to identify risks and implement mitigations
- Advises Product Managers on user, data, and product implications of security or privacy decisions
- Coordinates with Project Managers on risk tracking, escalation, and release readiness

---

## Operations / Site Reliability Engineer

### Role Summary
The Operations or Site Reliability Engineer supports reliable, observable, and recoverable services in production.

### Responsibilities
- Define operational readiness needs, including monitoring and alerting
- Advise on deployment, rollback, capacity, and reliability considerations
- Support incident response and operational learning after release

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Coordinates with Developers and the Engineering Lead on operability and production risks
- Works with Project Managers on deployment dependencies and release readiness
- Partners with QA on operational smoke tests and with Security on relevant production controls

---

## Customer Support / Success

### Role Summary
Customer Support or Success represents customer-facing needs and helps prepare teams to support delivered changes.

### Responsibilities
- Share recurring customer questions, feedback, and impact with the project team
- Prepare support guidance, training, and escalation information for launches
- Relay post-release issues and customer outcomes to inform follow-up work

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Partners with Product Managers to share customer insights and inform product priorities
- Coordinates with Project Managers on launch communications and support readiness
- Works with Developers and QA to route, clarify, and validate customer-reported issues
